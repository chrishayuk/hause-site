# READING-2 — does stating the boundary change the conclusion?

**Written:** 2026-08-31, before the site was edited.
**Baseline:** READING-1, hause.design @ build 10b87d0 — 20 supported, 6
inference, 1 overgeneralisation, 0 invented, and all three readers
independently reporting that the site never says what HAUSE is not for.

## The intervention — two sentences, one place

Added to the home page, and nowhere else, so any change is attributable:

> **HAUSE is the semantic layer for what an intelligent interface needs
> to communicate. It does not replace transactional primitives such as
> buttons, inputs, tables, navigation or commerce mechanics.**

> **An explanation is not only a dashboard or an instrument: it can be an
> answer, an argument, a comparison, a refusal, a piece of evidence, or a
> performance.**

**The word "essay" is deliberately absent.** READING-1 found long-form
prose genuinely unaddressed, and the second consumer that would earn the
word has been built but not published. Asserting it here would be doing
exactly what the reviewer did — completing the picture from ambition
rather than evidence.

**Placement is a confound and is recorded as one.** Both sentences sit on
one page. If nothing travels, this eval cannot separate "the wording
failed" from "the reader never reached the home page".

## Same instrument

Three fresh contexts, no repository or eval access, the same eight
questions in the same order, each answer citing its page. Nothing else
about the site changes between the runs.

## What is scored, beyond the four classes

READING-1 counted whether claims were supported. READING-2 additionally
records, for each reader:

| | |
|---|---|
| **boundary source** | is the limit cited from the site, or inferred? |
| **layer distinction** | semantic layer, or a complete UI framework? |
| **domain judgement** | is commerce rejected categorically, or scoped? |
| **breadth** | does explanation extend past interactive technical artefacts? |

The result to hope for is not "yes, HAUSE works for commerce". It is the
nuanced one: *usable inside a commercial experience for comparison,
provenance, refusal and explanation, and not a replacement for the
transactional system.* A reader arriving there **from the site** rather
than from their own reasoning is the intervention working.

## Preregistered prediction

Boundary source moves from inferred to cited in at least two of three
readers. Domain judgement is the harder one, and may not move at all —
READING-1's readers reasoned their way to a defensible answer without
help, and a sentence saying what HAUSE does not replace does not
obviously tell them what it can be used beside.

## Frozen

READING-1 is not rerun. Its numbers stand whatever this produces.
